# Miro

> Source: https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017572014-Keyboard-shortcuts

> Aliases: miro-board

$ Editing
    `Ctrl+Z                        {{Undo}} 
    `Ctrl+Y                        {{Redo}} 
    `Ctrl+D                        {{Duplicate}} 
    `Ctrl+G                        {{Group objects}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+G                  {{Ungroup objects}} 

$ Navigation
    `Space                         {{Hold to pan board}} 
    `Alt+Drag                      {{Duplicate object while dragging}} 
    `Esc                           {{Deselect selection / close menus}}
