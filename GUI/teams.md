# Microsoft Teams

> Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/keyboard-shortcuts-in-microsoft-teams-95f7fefa-2f5a-435e-a6b5-5f6d70c90b4f

> Aliases: microsoft-teams

$ General
    `Ctrl+E                        {{Go to Search}} 
    `Ctrl+1                        {{Open Activity}} 
    `Ctrl+2                        {{Open Chat}} 
    `Ctrl+3                        {{Open Teams}} 
    `Ctrl+4                        {{Open Calendar}} 
    `Ctrl+N                        {{Start a new chat}} 

$ Calls & Meetings
    `Ctrl+Shift+M                  {{Mute / unmute}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+O                  {{Toggle camera}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+K                  {{Raise / lower hand}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+A                  {{Accept incoming call}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+D                  {{Decline incoming call}}
