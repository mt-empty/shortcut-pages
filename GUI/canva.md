# Canva

> Source: https://www.canva.com/learn/keyboard-shortcuts/

> Aliases: canva-design

$ Editing
    `Ctrl+Z                        {{Undo}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+Z                  {{Redo}} 
    `Ctrl+D                        {{Duplicate selection}} 
    `Ctrl+G                        {{Group elements}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+G                  {{Ungroup elements}} 

$ Tools
    `T                             {{Add text}} 
    `V                             {{Select tool}} 
    `R                             {{Rotate tool}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+S                  {{Show keyboard shortcuts menu}}
