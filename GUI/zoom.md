# Zoom

> Source: https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/360031518471-Keyboard-shortcuts-for-Zoom-Meetings

> Aliases: zoom-meetings

$ Meeting Controls
    `Alt+A                         {{Mute / unmute audio}} 
    `Alt+V                         {{Start / stop video}} 
    `Alt+S                         {{Start / stop screen share}} 
    `Alt+Y                         {{Raise / lower hand}} 
    `Alt+Q                         {{Leave meeting}} 

$ Chat & Navigation
    `Alt+H                         {{Show / hide chat}} 
    `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H              {{Show / hide meeting controls}} 
    `Alt+N                         {{Switch camera}} 
    `Alt+U                         {{Show / hide participants panel}}
