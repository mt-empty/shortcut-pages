# Notion

> Source: https://www.notion.so/help/keyboard-shortcuts

> Aliases: notion-app

$ Navigation
    `Ctrl+P                        {{Open quick find / go to page}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+L                  {{Toggle light / dark mode}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+F                  {{Search page content}} 

$ Editor
    `Ctrl+B                        {{Bold}} 
    `Ctrl+I                        {{Italic}} 
    `Ctrl+U                        {{Underline}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+8                  {{Turn into bulleted list}} 
    `Ctrl+Shift+9                  {{Turn into numbered list}}
